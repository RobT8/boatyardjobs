# Marine / boatyard employer outreach targets (USA)

> **Purpose:** a prioritised prospect list for employer outreach — companies to
> invite onto BoatyardJobs.com (paid postings for the big multi-hire operators;
> the *"free posting / 6-months-free advertising for a careers-page backlink"*
> hook for the long tail). Prioritised by **hiring volume** — how many roles a
> prospect is likely to post — which is the best proxy for customer value.
>
> Compiled 2026-07-12 from public web research. **Read the email caveat below
> before you send anything.**

## ⚠️ Read this about the emails

Large marine employers almost never publish a `jobs@` inbox — they route hiring
through an **ATS careers portal** (Workday/ADP/UKG/iCIMS). So for the big
operators the realistic "contact" is the **careers page + a named HR person +
the company's email-format pattern** (build the address, then verify). Small
independents are the opposite: they publish a real `info@` on their Contact
page, and they're the ones most likely to jump on **free** posting.

Confidence tags used below:
- ✅ **verified** — address published on the company's own site / a primary source.
- 🔧 **pattern** — construct from the company's known format (e.g. `f.last@…`), then verify.
- 🌐 **portal** — no public email; apply/contact via the careers page or its web form.

Don't mass-mail constructed addresses blind — a bounce or a spam flag burns the
domain. Confirm on the site or via a tool (RocketReach/Hunter/Apollo) first.

## Tier 5 — The long tail: small independents (the "free advertising" audience)

Thousands of independent boatyards, marinas, marine-repair shops, mobile
mechanics, riggers, canvas/upholstery, detailers and small dealers. Individually
they post 1–3 jobs, but **they publish a real email on their site** and are the
segment most likely to take up **free posting + a "We're Hiring" badge backlink**
(the SEO/outreach play already in the handover). Rather than list every one, mine
them in bulk from these directories, then harvest the per-site `info@`/`contact@`:

**Marine trade / operator directories (best sources):**
- **ABBRA** — American Boat Builders & Repairers Assoc. member directory: `abbra.org/membership/directory` *(boatyards & repairers — exactly our niche)*
- **MRAA** — Marine Retailers Assoc. industry directory: `mraaindustrydirectory.com` *(free basic listings incl. name/address/phone)*
- **AMI** — Association of Marina Industries: `marinaassociation.org` *(marina operators)*
- **Marine Recreation Association**: `marina.org`
- **NMMA B2B directory**: `nmma.org/membership/directory`

**Regional / listing directories (per-business contact pages):**
- New England Boatbuilders — marinas/boatyards/repair: `newenglandboatbuilders.org/marinas-boatyards-repair.php`
- `by-the-sea.com` (full-service marinas & boatyards) · `boatersbook.com` · `marineandboat.directory` · `boatplanet.com/services` · `boatbroke.com` · `searchshop.com/boat/dealers`

**Bulk approach that works:** for each coastal/lake state, pull "boatyard /
marina / marine repair / boat dealer" from the directories above + Google Maps,
then scrape the Contact-page email. State marine-trade associations (FL, CA, TX,
NC, WA, MI, MD, NY, NJ, SC) publish member lists too. Prioritise businesses that
already show open roles on Indeed/their own site — they have live demand.
